# Visual Direction

## Feeling

Premium intelligence desk. Calm, editorial, serious, not a debate platform.

## Visual mood

- Dark elegant background
- Soft light beams or subtle gradient atmosphere
- Large typography
- Thin dividers
- No noisy cards everywhere
- Evidence shown as calm source strips
- Report feels like an intelligence brief

## Avoid

- Loud debate visuals
- Red vs blue boxing match layout
- Meme style
- Generic SaaS cards everywhere
- Overuse of icons

## Landing page hero copy

Every story has another side.

Paste a claim, article, post, or historical narrative. OtherSide AI shows the counter-story without judging who is right.

## UI sections

### Hero
Large headline, atmospheric background, one paste preview floating on page.

### How it works
Three elegant steps:

1. Paste one side
2. Detect the other party
3. Read the counter-story

### Modes
Use cinematic panels, not equal boring cards:

- Quick Counter
- Deep Dispute
- History Mirror

### Report page
The report should feel like a premium case brief.

Sections should blend vertically with subtle lines, not heavy boxes.
